Contact

Please send updates on ANY of the information here. I count on all of you to get me the latest information. Use this form to add a club, update a listing, share your experience of a club, or get in touch with me. Messages come straight to me by email and are not published.

What is this about?

In the message, tell me about the aircraft (with hourly rates, wet or dry, Hobbs or tach), join fee and how much is refundable, monthly dues, experience required, whether non-owned insurance is required, membership cap and wait list, and whether Mexico trips are allowed.
