Questions to ask a flying club

From the Arizona Flying Clubs guide: arizonaflyingclubs.com

Club: Date visited:

Crucial questions that are often unasked

  1. Who makes the big decisions? Is it a board vote, or do all members get a vote?

    Some pilots prefer to be hands-off, while others prefer to be involved in fleet, maintenance and financial decisions.

  2. Do the airplanes have any significant damage history? Have there been any incidents or accidents in the last 5 years that were reported to the FAA or the FSDO?

    Airplanes with structural damage that has been repaired may have non-standard flight characteristics, and may not be as safe.

  3. What is the total time of the airframes?

    Most pilots prefer to avoid airframes with more than 10,000 hours total time.

  4. Do all members pay the same monthly dues? Or do board members get a "free ride"?

    Officers at some of the clubs do not pay monthly dues. This increases the costs for members who are not on the board, and may have an impact on the officers' motivations when making decisions.

Money

  • What is the join fee, and how much of it is refundable equity when I leave? Can I resell my share?
  • What do the monthly dues cover (insurance, hangar, annual, databases)?
  • Is the hourly rate wet or dry? Is it on Hobbs or tach time?
  • Do I need to buy non-owned insurance, and how much coverage?
  • When did rates last go up? Is there a reserve for engine overhauls, or will there be special assessments?

Availability

  • How many members share each airplane? How far ahead do I need to book a weekend?
  • How hard is it to book the airplane? Ask for a screenshot of last month's online schedule.
  • Is Mexico or Canada allowed?
  • How does the club feel about long-distance and multi-day trips? Is there a minimum usage per day?
  • How much maintenance downtime did the airplane have last year?

Aircraft & joining

  • How old are the airframes and engines, and what are the times since overhaul?
  • Who does the maintenance, and how are squawks reported and fixed?
  • What experience is required, and what does the checkout involve? Are there CFIs in the club?
  • Is there a waiting list? Can I talk to a few current members, and see the airplane and the books?

Arizona Flying Clubs is an independent guide compiled by Theodore Dryden, last updated November 2026. Club details and prices change; always confirm with the club before joining.
