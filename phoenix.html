Flying Clubs in Phoenix

The Phoenix area has 12 flying clubs, based at Phoenix Deer Valley Airport, Chandler Municipal Airport, Falcon Field, Glendale Municipal Airport, Phoenix Goodyear Airport and Buckeye Municipal Airport, plus 5 pilot social clubs. Monthly dues run from $50 to $380, and the least expensive airplane at each club rents for $60 to $230 an hour, wet or dry depending on the club. Compare them below, then call the clubs that fit.

Traditional (Equity) Flying Clubs

100% non-profit, shared ownership: a shared airplane, shared insurance, and members vote on club and maintenance decisions. No non-owned insurance needed.

Non-Traditional Flying Clubs

The airplanes are not jointly or evenly owned by the members. Owners make the maintenance decisions and usually make a profit; non-owned insurance is almost always required (assume $500-1000 annually).
