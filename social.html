Social Flying Clubs

Social flying clubs don't own airplanes, hangars or anything else. They bring pilots together for hangar BBQs, fly-outs to interesting airports, breakfasts, and "hangar flying" sessions that build proficiency. Most are free or nearly free, and they are a great way to meet other pilots, hear which flying clubs are worth joining, and find people to share a cockpit with.
