Third Step Flying Club

Six IFR Piper Cherokees at Chandler for a yearly fee instead of monthly dues, with complex (Arrow) and tailwheel training available.

Notes

$360 a year: you pay the first year's $360 when you join, then $360 each year after. No separate join fee. Non-owned insurance required. Members also have access to a Piper Arrow for complex dual training and endorsement, and to tailwheel training and endorsement, through the club.

Aircraft

AircraftBasedRateNotes
6 Piper Cherokees CHD $128/hr wet All IFR certified, with upgraded engines, Powerflow exhaust, GPS, new nav/coms with glideslope, ADS-B in and out, and upgraded interiors. Five have standard six-pack panels; the sixth is being set up as a TAA (dual Dynon glass with autopilot). Block rates down to $102/hr wet, 100 hours.

  • Lowest cost of flying
  • Local flights and day trips
  • Instrument flying
  • Lower-performance aircraft
  • Building time
