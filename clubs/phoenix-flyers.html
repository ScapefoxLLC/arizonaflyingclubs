Phoenix Flyers

Four airplanes, including two G1000 Skylanes, rotating between Deer Valley and Chandler.

Notes

Refundable equity 5750. Airplanes rotate between DVT and CHD. Private Pilots minimum. 60 members. Mexico flights not allowed. Wait list.
